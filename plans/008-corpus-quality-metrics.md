## Metric contract

Implement streamed full-vocabulary comparison of aligned FP32 logits. Validate the frozen corpus and both capture identities/coverage before metrics. Each row is joined by split/document/position/input/next-token IDs and vocabulary; no directory-wide best-effort aggregation. Compute stable log-softmax in FP32 (subtract row maximum before logsumexp), then KL(p_reference||p_candidate) and NLL sums in FP64. Mean values are token-weighted. PPL ratio is exp(mean candidate NLL minus mean reference NLL), not an average of document perplexities. Compute nearest-rank p99, top1 agreement and category results.
